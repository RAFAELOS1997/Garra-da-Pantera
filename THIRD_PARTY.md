# Third-party components and models

HairSeparator AI depends on the Python packages listed in `requirements.txt`. Their licenses remain with their respective authors.

The optional image-analysis feature downloads the Hugging Face model `mattmdjaga/segformer_b2_clothes` at runtime. Review its current model card and license before redistribution or commercial deployment. Model weights are never committed to this repository.

Research references include CGAL, libigl, MeshLab/VCGlib, MeshLib, PyMeshFix, SegFormer and Self-Correction for Human Parsing. No source code from those projects is copied into this repository. Implementations here were written independently from documented algorithms and public interfaces.
